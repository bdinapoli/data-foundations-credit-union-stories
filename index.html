How credit unions are turning fragmented data into their greatest competitive advantage

Member expectations are rising. Many credit unions are navigating the same questions: how to connect data across systems, speed up member-facing processes, and build a foundation that supports AI. Below are a few peers who've been on that journey.

★ Dreamforce 2026 (Sept 15–17, San Francisco): Featured sessions from credit union customers sharing their data transformation stories, highlighting two below: PenFed and Wescom Financial.
Dreamforce 2026 Speaker
Nation's 2nd largest CU · 2.9M+ members · Serves veterans & military nationwide · Better Service in Banking Starts With AI

PenFed's members expected fast, personalized service but data lived in disconnected systems, making it hard for agents to get a single, complete member view. Routine questions consumed agent time that could go toward complex cases. PenFed built a unified member data platform and layered in AI-powered service then Olivia Boles, VP of Solution Architecture & Platform Engineering, shared the story on the Dreamforce main stage.

20% of cases resolved on first contact via AI
31% increase in membership
8.3% reduction in average handle time
PenFed's session "Better Service in Banking Starts With AI" highlighted how agentic AI is scaling operations without adding headcount, and why building reusable AI components is the key to sustained ROI.
Dreamforce 2026 Speaker
$6.5B assets · 250,000+ members · Pasadena, CA · Founded 1934 · Dreamforce session · Live session video (Salesforce+)

Wescom's ~50,000 monthly member calls were handled by reps jumping between 35+ siloed systems mid-conversation. Call summaries were manual, quality reviews sampled a fraction of calls. CIO Joseph Pellissuri set a mandate: "Show me that you know me." After unifying all systems into a trusted 360° member view, they layered in AI and the results were immediate.

35+ siloed systems unified into one member view
416 hrs saved monthly in the contact center
100% of calls auto-scored for quality with AI
77% improvement in contact center staff retention
"AI can only be as relevant and as accurate as the data that's being fed to it. Trusted data isn't a prerequisite for AI it's the whole game." Desigan Reddi, VP of IT Operations & Architecture, Wescom Financial

State Employees' Credit Union (SECU)
2nd largest CU in the US · 2.9M members · 1 in 4 North Carolina residents · Case study

SECU built individual point-to-point connectors for every integration creating inconsistency, security gaps, and a development backlog that slowed time to market. Members calling about locked cards generated high call volumes that could be solved digitally. SECU needed to move from reactive IT to an agile, member-first platform.

4 wks from idea to live API
~15 sec to member self-serve access
0 sec access to employee services (down from hours)
"We saw an immediate uptick in use before we'd even announced the feature. It also triggered a shift in behavior — members use it as a toggle to keep their cards secure when they're not using them." David Redd, IT Team Manager & SVP of Enterprise Applications, SECU
Marine Credit Union
$1.1B assets · 78,000 members · Wisconsin

Marine needed faster loan decisioning and account opening to compete on member experience. Manual steps and disconnected systems their CRM, loan platform, and core banking meant slow approvals and staff spending hours on swivel-chair work between screens.

55% of loan decisions now automated
350/day loan transactions processed
$1M annual interest income from integrated workflows
By connecting their CRM, loan origination platform, and core banking in real time, Marine eliminated swivel-chair work, accelerated access to member data, and unlocked $1M in additional annual interest income.
Idaho Central Credit Union
$6.7B assets · 432,000 members · Serves 1 in 4 people in Idaho

Guided by a mission to "help members achieve financial success," Idaho Central needed to bring new digital services to market faster. Every new service required custom, one-off integrations slowing the team and accumulating technical debt that made each subsequent project even harder.

92% reduction in member onboarding time
100% API reuse on new consumer loan booking service
432K members benefiting from faster digital services
With a reusable integration foundation in place, Idaho Central now brings entirely new digital services account opening, document signing, loan booking to 432,000 members without starting from scratch each time.
